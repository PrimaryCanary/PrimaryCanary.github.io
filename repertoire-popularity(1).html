How the ranking works

Each repertoire line is truncated after the requested number of moves. The resulting position is queried against the Lichess Opening Explorer, and lines are ranked by the number of games reaching that position under the selected rating/time-control filters.

For example, with 10 moves selected, the app scores the position after the tenth individual move in each repertoire line. Lines that have not reached that depth are omitted.
